**Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Branch:** `feat/optimiq-pbx-phase0` · **Method:** code-verified, not plan-verified

This document answers one question for every capability line in the two frozen inventories
(`plans/reference/freeswitch-capabilities.md`, `plans/reference/fusionpbx-inventory.md`): **what is
the current implementation state in this repository?**

**Read this as: 34% done, 22% half-done, 42% absent.** The distribution is extremely uneven and
that is the most important fact in this document. The **control plane and the routing brain are the
strongest parts of the system** — `packages/routing`, `packages/telephony`, `apps/api` CRUD and
`apps/web` are at or near the quality bar the plan set, with deep test suites and unusually honest
self-documentation. **The SIP edge and the media plane are the weakest**, and both are still
Asterisk with two incomplete Go replacements beside them.

Two structural facts frame everything below:

1. **Asterisk 22 is still the production SIP and media engine.** `compose.yaml:192` runs it;
   `compose.yaml:231` points the engine at `ARI_URL: http://asterisk:8088`;
   `apps/engine/src/media/ari.module.ts` defaults `ENGINE_MEDIA_DRIVER` to `ari`. **`apps/sipd` is
   not a service in `compose.yaml` or `compose.dev.yaml` at all and has no Dockerfile** — the only
   mentions are prose comments claiming it "is the SIP edge now". It is not deployed.
2. **A recurring failure shape: the type exists, is tested, and nothing calls it.** This is not
   speculation — it recurs in at least nine places, each named in the rows below
   (`aggregateDeviceState`, `BridgeMode`, `TrunkAttempt.weight`, `TrunkAttempt.maxChannels`,
   `recordings.retention_until`, `sipAclEntry`, `userSetting`, `DialVerb.progressTimeoutMs`,
   `TransferRequest.fallbackDestination`). Each is a compiled or declared decision that the runtime
   silently discards. **A field that lies in an artifact is worse than an absent one**, and this is
   the single highest-leverage class of defect to sweep.
