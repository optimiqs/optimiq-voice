# GAP2026 — the definitive 2026 gap list for a multi-tenant business calling platform

Scope: UCaaS + contact-centre basics, Telnyx as the only carrier. Baseline: HEAD `aaf6ff1`, working tree
clean (`git status --porcelain` shows only untracked `output/`), so nothing from the three in-flight packs
has landed yet. Every row below was verified by reading the tree at this commit, not by trusting
`CAPABILITY-MATRIX.md` — that matrix predates the September closure round and is stale in ~25 places
(backups, `/metrics`, CDR retention scheduling, porting, CNAM, hot desking, per-device Ray Baum, E.164
normalisation, agent/volume reporting, queue callback, attended transfer, recording pause/resume,
STIR/SHAKEN verstat capture and encrypted SSO secrets are all now PRESENT).

Three packs are already being implemented by other agents. Their rows are marked **IN PROGRESS** and are
not re-planned here:

- (a) recording compliance — consent announcement/policy, consent logging, jurisdiction policy, erasure workflow
- (b) contact centre — disposition codes, RONA, whisper/barge, agent whisper announcement, post-call survey, skills
- (c) security/fraud — TLS 1.3 minimum, cert reload, mTLS trunks, spend/velocity caps, geo blocks, anomaly alerts

## Ranked list — P0 and P1 **not** covered by the three running packs

**P0 — must have for a 2026 launch**

1. **1.1 SMS/MMS two-way messaging (L)** — the one feature every 2026 UCaaS buyer's checklist opens with, and the platform has none of it.
2. **1.2 10DLC brand + campaign registration (L)** — unregistered A2P traffic is blocked by the carriers; 1.1 cannot ship without it.
3. **1.4 Outbound STIR/SHAKEN attestation decisioning + number right-to-use (M)** — the April 2026 FNPRM makes the attestation decision the end-user provider's regardless of who signs; today nothing decides one, and the level that _is_ captured inbound is never shown to anyone.
4. **2.1 Traceback query path (M)** — a 24-hour clock the FCC has already enforced against 12 providers, and every CDR index leads with `organization_id`, so the cross-tenant question cannot be asked efficiently at all.
5. **2.2 Tenant KYC record (M)** — a documented know-your-customer process is now a certification, and the platform captures nothing beyond a billing email.
6. **3.5 Audit trail on recording/voicemail access and on recording pause/resume (S)** — PCI requirement 10 and the first question any DPA asks; deletion is audited, access and the PCI pause control are not.
7. **3.6 Voicemail and audit-log retention, and a CDR retention default that is not "never" (M)** — three of four data classes grow without bound today, which is a storage-limitation finding waiting to happen.

**P1 — expected in year one**

- **3.4 Recording/voicemail encryption at rest (M)**

**P2 — differentiators**: 1.5 delegate certificates · 5.7 AI post-call summary and suggested disposition · 5.8 queue ETA · 5.9 ring-group round-robin/memory · 6.3 vendor RPS zero-touch registration · 6.4 mobile clients · 1.9 libphonenumber for international.

### The shape of what is left

The call path is finished and measured; almost nothing on this list is a telephony gap. What remains
clusters into three kinds of work: **a missing product surface** (messaging, and the 10DLC/toll-free
registration it legally requires), **the evidence layer** — traceback queries, KYC records, attestation
decisions, access audit, bounded retention, tracing — which is what turns a working platform into one
that can answer a regulator, and **the last mile on things already built** (a screen-pop template over an
existing ringing webhook, a per-trunk field over an existing SRTP engine, an RPS client over an existing
provisioning renderer). Only the first is genuinely large.
